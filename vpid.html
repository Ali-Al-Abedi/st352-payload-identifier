Authoritative references used by this tool:
  • SMPTE ST 352:2013 — Payload ID base structure (Annex B for SD, Annex C historical)
  • SMPTE ST 292-1:2018 — HD-SDI 720/1080-line (Byte 1 = 0x84, 0x85)
  • SMPTE ST 292-2:2011 — Stereoscopic 720/1080-line on 1.5G HD-SDI; byte 2/3/4 layout reused by ST 425-2 (Byte 1 = 0x8E, 0x8F)
  • SMPTE ST 372:2017 — Dual-link 1.5G HD (Byte 1 = 0x87)
  • SMPTE ST 425-1:2017 — 3G-SDI Level A & B (Byte 1 = 0x88, 0x89, 0x8A, 0x8B–0x8D)
  • SMPTE ST 425-2:2012 — Stereoscopic 720/1080-line on single 3 Gb/s SDI (Byte 1 = 0x8E, 0x8F)
  • SMPTE ST 425-3:2019 — Dual-link 3G-SDI (Byte 1 = 0x94, 0x95, 0x96)
  • SMPTE ST 425-4:2012 — Stereoscopic 720/1080-line on dual-link 3G-SDI (Byte 1 = 0x91, 0x92, 0x93)
  • SMPTE ST 425-5:2019 — Quad-link 3G-SDI 4K (Byte 1 = 0x97, 0x98)
  • SMPTE ST 425-6:2014 — Stereoscopic 1080-line / 2160-line on quad-link 3G-SDI (Byte 1 = 0x99, 0x9A, 0x9B)
  • SMPTE ST 2081-10:2015 — 6G-SDI 4K/HFR (Byte 1 = 0xC0, 0xC1) — via Tektronix "Creating 4K/UHD Content" poster, not from the original SMPTE PDF
  • SMPTE ST 2082-10:2018 — 12G-SDI 4K/HFR (Byte 1 = 0xCE, 0xCF)
  • SMPTE ST 2082-11:2019 — Dual-link 12G-SDI 8K (Byte 1 = 0xD0)
  • SMPTE ST 2082-12:2019 — Quad-link 12G-SDI 8K (Byte 1 = 0xD2)
  • SMPTE ST 435-1:2012 — 10 Gb/s SDI Basic Stream Derivation; defines quad-link 1080-line and octa-link 2160-line VPID byte 2/3/4 layouts (Byte 1 = 0x90, 0xA0). ST 435-2 (Mode A/B/C/D multiplexing) and ST 435-3 (optical fibre interface) are physical-layer companions and don't affect VPID bytes 2-4.
  • SMPTE ST 2036-3:2018 / ITU-R BT.2077-1 — UHDTV1 / UHDTV2 over single-, dual-, quad- and octa-link 10G-SDI Mode D (Byte 1 = 0xA1, 0xA2)
  • SMPTE ST 2036-4:2019 / ARIB STD-B58 / ITU-R BT.2077-2 — UHDTV1 / UHDTV2 over multi-link 10G-SDI 12-bit container (Byte 1 = 0xA5, 0xA6) — note: colorimetry-bit polarity is INVERTED (1 = Rec. 709)
  • SMPTE ST 2047-2:2010 — VC-2 mezzanine compressed 1080p over 1.5 Gb/s HD-SDI (Byte 1 = 0xB0)
  • SMPTE Payload ID Registry — smpte-ra.org Payload Identifier Registry (used for full Byte 1 coverage; codes without a bit-level decoder show their registered standard, description, and status)

Bit-level definitions cite their originating standard on each decoded field, so any value can be traced to its source. Fields decoded from 6G-SDI VPIDs are marked with the citation ST 2081-10 (Tek) to distinguish them from fields confirmed against the official SMPTE PDFs. Codes signalled in the SMPTE-RA registry but without a published bit layout in this tool are flagged as "Recognised by registry — bit-level decoder not yet implemented". Round-trip integrity is verified by automated tests against known-good VPIDs.
